Battlegrounds

Three five-a-side battles between the factions.

Queue at Iselle Crane in Harrowford for any of the three battlegrounds. Wins and losses both earn honour, which buys battle-forged gear and Champion's Tabard of the Shattered Hall.

Battle bots take the places no hero fills. A blow from one on a hero is halved, just as a hero's blow on another hero is (1.14.86). When the other side has more heroes than yours, your side's bots rally: each has more life for every hero more, 40% more at level 15 and below, rising to 70% more from level 35. Everyone in the battle is told when a side rallies, a bot that gets up after a fall comes back rallied, and nobody rallies when both sides have the same number of heroes. A bot resists fire, cold and lightning about as much as its armour takes off a blow: 15%, or 20% from level 40, so spells land on it as blades do (1.14.90).

A fallen hero waits as long as a bot does before getting up (1.14.88). The button on the death screen counts the seconds down; press it early and you get up as soon as the wait is over.

The Kiln Road (1.14.85) is fought over a wain rather than a place: whichever side has more heroes round it moves it toward the other side's toll gate. The road climbs to a crest in the middle, so a wain pushed off the crest rolls down the far side by itself. Past a milestone a side's forward camp opens, so its fallen rise nearer the fight. The nearer the wain comes to a side's gate, the longer that side's fallen take to get up, up to twice as long by the gate, so a side cannot hold it a few metres short forever; and when as many of each side stand round it, it rolls slowly the way of the side winning the fight (1.14.88). A strip on screen shows where the wain stands on the road, and the scoreboard counts each hero's deliveries and metres driven.

The Shattered Hall

5 v 5

The Lantern Compact and the Thorn Covenant settle an old grudge in the ruins of a Weaver hall. Hold its three hearthstones to gather renown: the first side to 1000 wins.

The Sunken Reliquary

5 v 5

Beneath a drowned chapel the Tidewardens and the Emberwatch each keep a reliquary. Carry the other side's relic home to your own while yours is safe on its stand: the first side to carry three home wins.

The Kiln Road

5 v 5

On the old road out of the Cinderreach the Lantern Compact and the Emberwatch fight over a wain of kiln-glass. The wain rolls toward the gate of the side outnumbered round it: drive it through the other side's toll gate. The nearer the wain comes to a side's gate, the longer that side's fallen take to get up. The first side to drive it home twice wins.

Teams
The Lantern Compact against The Emberwatch
Team size
5 a side
Time limit
15 minutes
Respawn
8 seconds

The wain
Rolls toward the gate of the side outnumbered within 6 m of it, faster the bigger the margin; slow uphill, quick downhill; when the numbers are even it rolls slowly the way of the side with more life between them (1.14.88)
Getting up
Slower the nearer the wain is to your own gate, up to twice the respawn by the gate (1.14.88)
After a delivery
It rests 10 seconds, then goes back to the crest
Out of time
The side with more deliveries wins; if level, the side whose half of the road the wain stands on
Honour
40 for each delivery your side makes, and 2 for every 10 metres you help drive it
