Battlegrounds

Five-a-side battles between the factions.

Queue at Iselle Crane in Harrowford. Wins and losses both earn honour, which buys battle-forged gear and Champion's Tabard of the Shattered Hall.

The Shattered Hall

5 v 5

The Lantern Compact and the Thorn Covenant settle an old grudge in the ruins of a Weaver hall. Hold its three hearthstones to gather renown: the first side to 1000 wins.

Teams
The Lantern Compact against The Thorn Covenant
Team size
5 a side
Time limit
15 minutes
Respawn
8 seconds
Brackets
Every 10 levels
Empty places
Filled by bots after 60 seconds in the queue
Goal
First to 1000 renown
Hearthstones
the Bell Hearth, the Anvil Hearth, the Well Hearth
Capture
8 seconds standing on a hearthstone

The Sunken Reliquary

5 v 5

Beneath a drowned chapel the Tidewardens and the Emberwatch each keep a reliquary. Carry the other side's relic home to your own while yours is safe on its stand: the first side to carry three home wins.

Teams
The Tidewardens against The Emberwatch
Team size
5 a side

Respawn
8 seconds

Goal

Relics
the Tide Reliquary, the Ember Reliquary
