Festivals

Lanternfast, the midwinter feast: when it runs, the fair at Harrowford, its daily tasks, the Unlit and the Candlewright's wares.

Festivals come at the same time every year, by the calendar on the server (UTC). While one runs its people stand in town, the towns are dressed for it, and its tasks and wares are there for heroes of any level; when it ends they go until next year. The first is Lanternfast (1.14.87), the midwinter feast of the long night.

Lanternfast

From 20 November to 6 January

The midwinter feast of the long night, when every hearth lights a lantern against the dark. It comes every year from 20 November to 6 January (server time, UTC). While it runs every town is dressed for it: strings of paper lanterns over the streets and round every square, wreaths by the doors, garlands along the eaves, and a Lantern Tree on each square, hung with lanterns and gift parcels and glowing at night. The fair itself keeps at Harrowford. The towns dress themselves the moment the feast begins at midnight, with a notice to everyone in the world, and undress the same way when it ends (1.14.88); nobody has to log in again to see it.

The fair at Harrowford

While the feast runs, Hesper Wick and Mabyn Tallow stand on Harrowford's square. Round each of the six towns, where the fields begin, six boundary lanterns stand on posts, one for each road the dark comes in by (every town since 1.14.98, Harrowford's alone before). Each town hangs them in its own way: a farm-fence stake standing in a hay bale at Millbrook, a crooked birch with its lantern under a copper hood at Lisswen, sea-bleached driftwood with the lantern on a loop of rope at Gullhaven, an iron brazier on a stone plinth at Emberwatch, and a stone post heaped with rime and hung with icicles at Highwatch. Each can be lit, or trimmed if it already burns, once a day by each hero, and a snuffed one goes dark. Light one in every town in one Lanternfast for the deed Every Hearth Alight. Night falls on the sky's own clock, which turns once every forty minutes, so it is dark for about nineteen minutes of each turn.

The Unlit

By night

Smoke round a wick that has gone out. At Lanternfast the Unlit come in from the dark for every light. After dark the Unlit come out of the dark at the boundary lanterns of whichever town has heroes near, in packs of three and never more than six at once at a town, at the level of the heroes there, and go for the lanterns that burn. One that stands by a burning lantern for a few seconds puts it out. Everyone who fights one gets a candle when it is driven off, and at dawn the rest thin away.

The Lamp-Eater

Once a night

An Unlit that has eaten so many lanterns their light still burns inside its smoke. It comes at the end of the night for whatever is left alight.

Once a night, a little after the midnight fireworks, the Lamp-Eater comes to one town's lanterns, a different town each night in turn through all six, if heroes are there. It is a great Unlit, an elite at the level of the heroes it finds, swollen with the lanterns it has swallowed, their light still glowing inside its smoke. It leads the last of the night's packs, and if it is left standing by a burning lantern it swallows the light, twice as fast as an Unlit snuffs one and from twice as far. It fades with the dawn if no one drives it off. Running into the lanes of a town is no escape: it follows a hero through any of the grown towns (1.14.102).

Everyone who fought it gets six Lanternfast candles when it is driven off, and the deed Nothing Left to Eat with the title Lampbane. Now and then it lets fall the Swallowed Lantern, a lantern scorched black with a cold blue flame, held in the off hand; it can't be bought.

The Old Chandlery

The feast's own dungeon

Under Harrowford's square run the cellars of the old chandlery, where the village's candle-makers dipped and stored the long-night lamps for a hundred winters: low brick vaults on timber posts, the dipping frames still hung with half-made candles, the tallow vats gone cold. The first lantern of the first Lanternfast was lit from a wick dipped here, and when at last it went out the chandlers carried the wick down and laid it in the deepest vault. The cellars were shut when the chandlery closed. This winter something has grown round that wick in the dark, and the Unlit come up the cellar steps every night.

While the feast runs, its cellar door stands open off Harrowford's square, beside the market. It is a group dungeon met at your party's own level, from 10 to 60, with friends or the finder's companions. At the bottom, in the dipping hall, The Hollow Wick stands among four great lanterns. It draws a breath at the lantern farthest from you and blows it out; stand by the flame and you hold it. Each lantern out makes it a fifth stronger and brings the Unlit out of the dark behind it. Light the lanterns again: with all of them burning the light goes right through it, and it reels for six seconds, taking half again as much from every blow. Below half its life it snuffs two at a breath and its choking smoke pools on the floor (1.14.89).

The Hush, Heart of the Long Night

The feast's world boss

On every night of the feast the dark gathers in The Gloaming, a hollow in the meadow south-west of Harrowford where the fair's lights never reach. Bring a light there and the Hush rises for it: a great Unlit, smoke gathered round a dead lantern, at the level of the heroes who came, from 10 to 60. Its spoils come once a day: 6 candles, gear of its level and gold, and now and then a Kindled Unlit. You can see when night falls in the Gloaming in the Raids window and on the map.

Fireworks at midnight

Every night of the feast

Every night of the feast, when the sky's clock reaches midnight (once every forty minutes), each of the six towns sends up about a minute of fireworks: rockets whistling up, bursts of lantern gold, red and cold white, and a big finale. The bang comes a moment later the farther away you stand, so from a hill you can watch one town's show and hear another's. On New Year's Eve, at midnight UTC, a three-minute show takes that night's place over every town, and when the Hush falls, twenty seconds of gold go up over the Gloaming for everyone who was there. Every player sees the same rockets at the same moment. There are no fireworks in dungeons or battlegrounds.

The Old Chandlery

Daily, from Hesper Wick

There's a cellar door by the market nobody's opened since the old chandlery shut. The chandlers dipped Harrowford's long-night lamps down there for a hundred winters, and they say the wick of the very first Lanternfast lantern is laid in the deepest vault. This winter the Unlit come up those steps, more of them every night, and I've heard something breathing in the dark under the square. Go down and put it out. If the great lanterns in the old hall go dark, light them again: whatever is down there can't abide them all burning. Take friends, or the hired swords. The Candlewright1

The Long Night garb and the lantern

Sold by Mabyn Tallow for candles

Mabyn Tallow takes candles, not gold. Candles come from Hesper Wick's tasks, the Unlit and the Lamp-Eater. The garb goes in your wardrobe like any other look, and a firework, right-clicked, sends a burst of lantern-gold sparks over your head for everyone near to see. A day's tasks with the Unlit fought along the way bring 17 or 18 candles, and the whole garb with the lantern costs 110, so about a week of tasks dresses you for the feast. From level 10 the Old Chandlery's task and the Hollow Wick add eleven more a day.
