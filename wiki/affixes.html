Affixes

The random properties that magic and rare items roll.

Magic items roll up to one prefix and one suffix; rare items up to three of each. The item level of a drop decides the best tier it can roll, and each tier asks for a hero level of four-fifths of its own item level; crafting a piece you are wearing rolls only tiers you can meet. "#" stands for the rolled number.
