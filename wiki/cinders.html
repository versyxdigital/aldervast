Cinders

The inns' dice game: the rules and scoring, the six regulars and how each plays, stakes, a table at home and the Champion of the Six Tables.

Cinders is the inns' dice game (1.14.106), played at a table in the corner of every inn against the inn's regular, your friends or anyone who'll sit down. Two to four play, with six dice, and the first to 6,000 points wins.

On your turn roll all six, then set aside at least one die that scores. Then either roll on with the rest, or bank the points you have built up this turn. If a roll has nothing that scores, the fire's out: you lose the turn's points and the dice pass on. Set aside all six and it is a full hearth: roll all six again, keeping what you've built. A set you keep must score in full, and dice only combine with others from the same roll. You have 25 seconds for each choice; keys 1 to 6 set a die aside, Space rolls on and B banks.

Talk to a regular to play a friendly game, or for 10, 25 or 50 gold. A regular pays one hero up to 300 gold a day, and after that still plays you, but only for fun. Stand within about 8 metres of a table and you'll see the dice land, and when a regular dares the dice, rolling on past where it would bank, the game says so. Ask a regular who is already in a game and you are next in line: watch the game, and you sit down the moment it ends; walk away from the table or close the window and you lose your place (1.14.107). Once a regular has paid you nearly all it will today, its card says how much more and which stakes are still open. Each innkeeper has a quest, “A Game at the Inn”: sit down for one game with their regular, win or lose. It pays a share of your own level, so it is worth doing whenever you arrive.

Every Furnisher sells a Cinders table for 400 gold for your room. Two to four of your friends or party can sit down at it, and the first to sit sets the stake, up to 500 gold. The table can't be moved or put away while a game is on, and a guest sent down from the room mid-game (struck off the owner's friends, or the owner leaves) has the game called off and gets the stake back (1.14.107). Beat all six regulars to earn the title Champion of the Six Tables and a trophy for your room, The Champion's Dice Cup. There are deeds for beating each regular, Six Tables for beating all six and A Hand at the Dice for winning 25 games.
