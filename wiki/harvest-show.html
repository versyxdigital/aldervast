The Harvest Show

Every Sunday on each town's square: the four classes, the judges and stewards, quality from Fair to Prize, rosettes, Show Tokens and the show stall, the quest and the deeds.

Every Sunday (1.14.116) each of the six towns holds its Harvest Show on the square. A striped marquee goes up, with show tables set out where the market's stalls stand on other days, and the town's two judges and its steward wait in the marquee. Bring what you grew at the allotments or something you cooked, and enter it in one of four classes: Best Vegetable, Best Fruit or Flower, Best Herbs and Best Dish. You can enter each class once a show, and your entry stands on the table for everyone to see. The town's own folk enter too.

The judges go round at 20:00 (server time), working through the six shows over the next half minute; while they do, entries are closed. What wins is quality: every crop comes up Fair, Good, Fine or Prize, by how it was kept, and a dish takes the quality of what went into it. A show variety is marked up by half a quality, and the judges' own fancy decides between equals. First, second and third in each class win a rosette, gold and Show Tokens; the rosettes stay on the tables until midnight. If you are not in the world at the judging, your prizes come by post. On other days the Show window keeps last Sunday's results for the town, with your own entries and places, and the date of the next show. Every entry earns 25 Gardening or Cooking experience and a Show Token, win or lose.

How a crop's quality comes about: a bed kept wet for nearly all of its growing (watered each time the soil dried) and free of weeds comes up Prize. One watered late once or twice comes up Fine, one watered now and then Good, and one never watered Fair. A crop lifted more than a day after it was ready drops a quality. The quality shows as stars on the item, and crops of different qualities stack apart.

Rosettes are keepsakes for your room at the inn. The steward keeps the show stall, which sells for Show Tokens the seed of five show varieties, a show cup for your room and a Show tabard. On a Sunday you are told of the shows when you enter the world, and at midnight as Sunday begins; the world map shows "Harvest Show today" under every town.

From level 6, after First Sowing, Nettie Dibble at Millbrook Farms sends you in Show and Tell to enter something at a Harvest Show, with a packet of Longfellow carrot seed for when your Gardening is up to it.

The deeds: Rosette (win a rosette (first, second or third) at a Harvest Show), Best in Class (come first in a class at a Harvest Show), A Rosette in Every Class (win a rosette in every class of the Harvest Show), Champion of the Six Towns (come first in a class at the Harvest Show of every town) and A Regular at the Show (enter twenty things at Harvest Shows).
