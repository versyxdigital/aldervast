Item sets

Sets whose pieces grant bonuses together.

Each class has a tier set from each raid, with bonuses at two and four pieces. The Spire of the Sundered Crown's level-30 sets come from its three bosses (the Rime Colossus gives the gloves or the boots), and the Drowned Basilica's level-40 sets one piece from each of its four encounters. The other sets drop anywhere their level allows.
