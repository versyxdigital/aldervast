How quests open

Start here

Quests are never random, and none waits on a dungeon or the Trial unless it says so. A test hero walks all of them the way a player does, so every one can be finished. A quest is offered when three things are true:

  1. Each quest it follows on from has been handed in, not just finished. A quest still waiting to be handed in holds the next one back.
  2. Your level is no more than three below the quest's. A level 15 quest can be taken from level 12.
  3. You are standing by the person who gives it, and your story has brought them there.
Marks
! over someone means they have a quest you can take now, and ? that you can hand one in to them; with both, the ? shows. The marks show on the minimap and the world map wherever the person is, so a quest waiting in another town shows from afar.
Where to go
Every goal of a quest you follow has a place, worked out by the game from where things really are: the person where your story has put them, the hunting ground where the monsters live at about the quest's level, a dungeon's door, or the spot or stretch where things lie. The minimap shades each place gold with a dashed rim (a gold diamond for a single spot), with an arrow on its rim for a goal beyond its edge. The world map rings the places with the quest's name, and hovering any of them names the goal.
Vigils
A vigil is sized to the heroes keeping it: one hero meets half of each wave, two meet three quarters and three or more the whole wave, never fewer than 3 monsters. A lone keeper's wave comes in two groups nine seconds apart, plain monsters with a magic champion, never a rare one. No open-world pack or story camp stands within 34 m of a vigil's altar.
Tracker and journal
Under each followed quest the tracker gives the place and how far it is ("Rootdeep Warrens · 130 m SE", or "here" once you are in it); once the quest is ready it points to whoever takes it back. The journal (J) gives each open goal's place and distance, and where to find whoever you hand a finished quest in to.
The main story
One quest at a time, in order: each opens when the one before it is handed in. The giver's words as you hand one in say where the story goes next.
Side quests
Up to level 27 most need only your level and a visit to the right town. Later ones each open with a step of the main story.
People who move
Wenna moves from place to place as the story goes on, and three people appear late in the story only for heroes who made a certain choice.
Daily duties
Each can be done once a day, and comes back at midnight UTC.
Dropping a quest
Abandon it in the journal (J) and you can take it again from the start.
