Market days

Travelling traders at each town's square on its market day: which town on which day, the fourteen traders, and what each trade brings and costs.

Travelling traders are on the roads (1.14.112), and every town has a market day of its own: one town a day, from midnight to midnight (UTC). On Sundays the traders are on the road, and there is no market.

DayMarketStallsPrices
MondaysMillbrook Farms4×1
TuesdaysLisswen Hollow4×1.2

On its market day the town's square is dressed: striped awnings over the stalls, each trader's wares on the counter and on the ground in front, bunting strung from stall to stall and a trader's hand cart. Three to five of the fourteen travelling traders, of seven trades, stand at the stalls, never two of one trade, and each brings a few of the trade's wares. Who comes and what they bring changes from week to week; it follows the date, so it is the same for every hero, on every server and in Single Player, all day. The traders wear the clothes of their trade, and more townsfolk come out to browse at the stalls (on the Low setting the town's own folk browse, and no extra ones come out). The next day the square is plain again until the round comes back.

Talk to a trader to buy, through the merchants' window. They sell things you can't buy anywhere else: dyers sell patterns for your wardrobe, joiners bring furniture made in the other towns, spice-sellers and cupmakers the other inns' suppers and drinks, anglers lures and bait, herbalists herbs and sickles, and toymakers pets in a basket. Prices follow the town, from the traders' own prices at Harrowford and Millbrook Farms to 2.1 times at Highwatch Keep, and some wares are limited to so many a hero each market. A trader doesn't bring what the town's own inn or shop already sells.

When you enter the world, a line in chat says which town has its market today, or where and when the next one is, and at midnight everyone in the world is told the new day's market. The world map shows a striped awning and "Market today" over the town holding its market; hover over any town to see its market day.

From level 4, Peg Hollin, Harrowford's Fair-Warden, gives the quest Market Day: visit a market and buy something, anything. The reward grows with your level, so it is worth taking whenever you first meet her, and your journal sends you to the nearest market today, or to the next one on a Sunday.

The deeds: Market Day (buy something from a travelling trader at a town's market), Six Markets (buy something at the market of every town, from Millbrook Farms to Highwatch Keep) and A Regular (buy fifty wares from the travelling traders).
